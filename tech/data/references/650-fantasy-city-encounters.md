650 hook di incontri urbani per campagne fantasy. Usare come ispirazione per plotting o come spunto istantaneo durante il gioco quando i personaggi fanno qualcosa di inaspettato.

## Encounters

1. The town guard think the party's mage is responsible for a magical duel fought atop the cathedral roof last night. Can the party find the real culprit before they are hunted down themselves?
2. The party is taken aside to the guardhouse upon first entering the city. The guard captain attempts to shake down the party under pain of death.
3. A woman falls into the street from the second story window and dies on impact. A PC notices a hooded figure skillfully dropping from another window into the alley.
4. The party is invited to a manor by a socialite for a job opportunity. They are locked in a room with a magical ward.
5. A lead brings you to a dive bar. Your suspect is upstairs slipping out the fire escape, guarded by three armed gangsters.
6. Upon returning to your room at the inn, you discover a body on the floor with a dagger. Guards storm up the stairs.
7. The party arrives at an empty temple. The entire congregation rushes in and takes the party down into the crypt.
8. Two groups of street fighters armed with rocks and sticks rush onto opposite sides of the street with the party caught in the middle.
9. The party stops to witness a funeral procession for a crime lord. An explosion and gigantic smoking fireball rise a block away.
10. In need of shelter, the characters come to a ruined tower full of revelling adventurers. The Rules of the House say no one may enter until they have entertained the rabble with a rousing tale.
11. A disheveled girl smashes into you. "Wilkenson's dog is loose again!" A metal construct resembling a dog charges toward you.
12. A pregnant woman walking through the bazaar quickly grabs a loaf of bread, favouring one of the PCs with a wink. Thief or wife of the store owner?
13. A residential door opens briefly and a high pitched scream emits from inside before the door suddenly closes. None of the other people on the street react.
14. The door bursts open. A well-armed man storms in: "Don't move, I've...crap, wrong room." He quickly exits.
